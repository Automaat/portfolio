# Dolomites Alpine Trek

The Alta Via 1 traverse through the Dolomites offered a week of stunning alpine scenery. The distinctive limestone towers and ridges of the Dolomites created endless compositional opportunities.

Autumn brought perfect hiking conditions and the warm glow of larch forests turning golden against the grey rock faces.
