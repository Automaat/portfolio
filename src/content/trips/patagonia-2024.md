# Patagonia Expedition

Three weeks of trekking through some of the most dramatic landscapes on Earth. From the iconic towers of Torres del Paine to the jagged peaks of Fitz Roy, this expedition showcased the raw, untamed beauty of Patagonia.

The weather was challenging, with strong winds and unpredictable conditions, but the dramatic light and constantly shifting clouds created incredible photographic opportunities.
