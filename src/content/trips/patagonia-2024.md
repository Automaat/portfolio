# Patagonia Expedition

Three weeks of trekking through some of the most dramatic landscapes on Earth. From the iconic towers of Torres del Paine to the jagged peaks of Fitz Roy, this expedition pushed the kit and the route harder than anything before it.

The weather was the brief, not the obstacle. Strong winds and unpredictable conditions created the dramatic light, and the constantly shifting clouds turned every viewpoint into a different photograph between morning and afternoon.

## Field approach

Lightweight system, two bodies, three lenses. Days spent walking; evenings spent reviewing what the weather had given. The goal was a library, not a postcard — frames that hold up across editorial, motion stills, and commercial usage.
