# Iceland Winter Journey

A winter expedition through Iceland's dramatic landscapes. From the black sand beaches of Reynisfjara to the ice caves of Vatnajökull, the brief was clear — capture the stark beauty of Iceland in winter without leaning on the obvious frames everyone already has.

## What worked

Three nights of clear skies handed back what felt like every aurora cliché in the book — and a few that didn't look like anyone else's. Daytime coverage focused on conditions: wind, snow, low light. Less postcard, more place.
