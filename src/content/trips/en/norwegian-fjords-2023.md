# Norwegian Fjords Sailing

Two weeks sailing through Norway's spectacular fjord system. The midnight sun gave back endless golden hour — useful, until everything started looking like a calendar shoot. The work was pulling out the moments that didn't.

From the colorful harbors of Bergen to the remote beauty of the Lofoten Islands, this leg combined the two parts of my work that overlap most directly: photography and the sea.
