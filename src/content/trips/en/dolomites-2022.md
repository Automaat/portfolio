# Dolomites Alpine Trek

The Alta Via 1 traverse offered a week of stunning alpine scenery. The distinctive limestone towers and ridges of the Dolomites created endless compositional opportunities — but the real challenge was sequencing them into something that read as a single piece, not a highlight reel.

Autumn brought perfect hiking conditions and the warm glow of larch forests turning golden against the grey rock faces.
