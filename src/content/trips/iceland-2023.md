# Iceland Winter Journey

A winter expedition through Iceland's dramatic landscapes. From the black sand beaches of Reynisfjara to the ice caves of Vatnajökull, this journey captured the stark beauty of Iceland in winter.

Multiple nights of aurora hunting rewarded us with spectacular displays of the northern lights dancing across the Arctic sky.
