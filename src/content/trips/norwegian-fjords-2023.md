# Norwegian Fjords Sailing

Two weeks sailing through Norway's spectacular fjord system. The midnight sun provided endless golden hour light, perfect for capturing the dramatic interplay between sea and mountain.

From the colorful harbors of Bergen to the remote beauty of the Lofoten Islands, this sailing expedition combined my two passions: photography and the sea.
