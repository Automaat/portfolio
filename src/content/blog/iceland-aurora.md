They say aurora photography is 90% patience, 10% skill. After my Iceland trip, I'd say it's more like 95% patience, 4% luck, and 1% knowing which button to press.

## Night One: Clouds

The forecast was perfect. KP index of 4, clear skies predicted. Reality: complete cloud cover. We drove 200km around the south coast, chasing breaks in the clouds. Nothing.

## Night Two: More Clouds

Repeat of night one. Different locations, same clouds. Started questioning life choices.

## Night Three: Magic

Third night's the charm, they say. At 2 AM, the clouds finally parted over Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon. What happened next was beyond anything I'd imagined.

The aurora exploded across the entire sky - greens, purples, even hints of red. They danced, pulsed, and swirled for over three hours. I shot over 500 frames and didn't want to miss a moment behind the viewfinder.

## Technical Notes

- **Exposure:** 10s, f/2.8, ISO 3200
- **Lens:** 14mm f/2.8
- **Patience Required:** 72 hours

Worth every frozen finger and sleepless night.
