Every sailor remembers their first solo night passage. Mine was supposed to be a straightforward 40-nautical-mile cruise along the Polish coast. The forecast: light winds, calm seas.

The reality was... different.

## Hour 1: Confidence

Departed Gdańsk at sunset with a gorgeous pink sky. Autopilot engaged, music playing, feeling like a proper sailor. This was going to be easy.

## Hour 3: Humility

Wind picked up from nowhere. Forecast said 10 knots. Anemometer reading: 25, gusting 30. Waves building. Time to reef.

Reefing solo at night in building seas is a special kind of character-building experience. Every line seems to tangle, every movement needs three hands.

## Hour 5: Respect

Proper night now. No moon. Just me, the instruments, and a very active Baltic Sea. Coffee lukewarm, nerves sharp. This is where preparation pays off.

Made it to Hel at 3 AM, exhausted but exhilarated.

## What I Learned

- **Check forecasts, but don't trust them completely**
- **Practice reefing in daylight before you need it at night**
- **Prepare hot food before departure** - you won't want to cook later
- **Respect the sea** - always

The sea doesn't care about your confidence. It only responds to your preparation.
