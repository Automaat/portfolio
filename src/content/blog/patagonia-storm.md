The morning started deceptively calm. We'd camped at the base of Torres del Paine, planning to summit for sunrise. By 4 AM, we were already halfway up when the wind hit.

## The Storm Arrives

Patagonian winds are legendary, but experiencing them firsthand is something else entirely. What started as a strong breeze quickly escalated to sustained winds of 100+ km/h, with gusts that could knock you off your feet.

We made the difficult decision to turn back. In the mountains, ego has no place when nature shows its power.

## Lessons Learned

This expedition taught me:

- **Respect the forecast** - Local knowledge isn't just helpful, it's essential
- **Turn back early** - The summit will still be there tomorrow
- **Trust your instincts** - If something feels wrong, it probably is

The next day, we returned to clear skies and successfully reached the summit. The torres were worth the wait, and the lesson was invaluable.
